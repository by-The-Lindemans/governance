# SUCCESSION AND CONTINUITY PLAN

*restore280 Institute — Maintained pursuant to Bylaws Section 9.8*

*Adopted by the Board of Directors by Written Consent, effective October 1, 2026*

*Reviewed at each annual governance review under Section 7.5, and within 15 days of notification of any material change in leadership or organizational structure.*

---

## 1. Purpose

This plan is maintained pursuant to Bylaws Section 9.8. It shall be expanded as the Corporation's staff and Board grow beyond their current size.

---

## 2. Executive Leadership Succession

### 2.1 Joint Office Holding

The Executive Director and Treasurer offices may be held by the same individual, as permitted by Bylaws Section 4.1. Where they are held jointly, the succession provisions of this Section 2 apply to both offices unless the Board appoints a separate Treasurer under Section 2.3(4).

### 2.2 Temporary Incapacity or Unavailability

If the Executive Director becomes temporarily unable to serve (illness, emergency, unreachability for a period the Board Chair judges material):

1. The Board Chair assumes interim operational authority for time-sensitive matters only: banking access sufficient to meet existing obligations, communication with the Corporation's registered agent and counsel, and any government filing with an imminent deadline.
2. The Board Chair may not, under this interim authority alone, commit the Corporation to any new expenditure above the Section 8.2 dual-authorization threshold, enter new partnerships, or make public statements on the Corporation's behalf beyond routine operational continuity.
3. The Board Chair notifies the remaining director(s) within 48 hours of invoking this interim authority.
4. Interim authority ends when the Executive Director resumes duties, or when the Board formally appoints an interim Executive Director under Section 2.3.

### 2.3 Extended Incapacity, Resignation, or Removal

If the Executive Director's absence extends beyond 30 days, or upon resignation or removal:

1. The Board shall appoint an interim Executive Director by majority written consent within 14 days, drawing first from existing directors, then from the Advisory Board if constituted, then from an external search if neither is available.
2. The interim Executive Director has full Section 4.2 authority, subject to ordinary Board oversight.
3. The Board shall begin a permanent replacement process within 60 days of the vacancy, unless the Board documents a reason for delay.
4. The Treasurer role, if it was held jointly with the Executive Director, is filled separately by Board resolution under Section 4.1 if no interim appointee is prepared to hold both.

## 3. Board Leadership Succession

### 3.1 Board Chair Vacancy

If the Board Chair position becomes vacant (resignation, removal, incapacity), the Board shall elect an acting Chair from among the remaining Independent Directors at the earliest practicable opportunity, consistent with Bylaws Section 3.10. Until that election, the most senior remaining Independent Director by length of service presides over any synchronous meeting and serves as the Board's point of contact.

### 3.2 Vacancy Threatening the Independence Requirement

If any director's departure, incapacity, or removal causes the Board to no longer satisfy the independence requirement of Bylaws Section 3.3, the remaining directors shall treat appointment of a qualifying replacement as the Board's first priority, ahead of any Board action other than actions necessary to preserve the Corporation's legal or financial standing. The remaining directors shall appoint a qualifying Independent Director under Bylaws Section 3.6 within 30 days of the vacancy. If no qualifying candidate is identified within that period, the remaining directors shall document the steps taken to date and shall continue treating the appointment as the Board's standing first priority until the requirement is restored.

### 3.3 Full Board Turnover

If more than one director seat becomes vacant simultaneously, the remaining directors shall fill vacancies under Bylaws Section 3.6, consistent with Section 3.2 where the resulting Board composition threatens the independence requirement.

## 4. Emergency Delegation of Authority

### 4.1 Designated Emergency Contacts

The Executive Director is the primary point of contact for banking and financial emergencies, legal and compliance emergencies, and governance repository administration; the Board Chair is the backup for each.

### 4.2 Bank Account Emergency Access

The Board Chair shall be added as a secondary authorized signer on the Corporation's operating account(s) once opened, subject to the same dual-authorization threshold that applies to the Executive Director under Bylaws Section 8.2. This authority is limited to the dual-authorization signer role already available under Section 8.2.

### 4.3 Scope of Emergency Authority

Emergency delegation under this Section is limited to actions necessary to prevent imminent harm to the Corporation (a missed filing deadline, an account access lockout, an unpaid obligation risking service interruption). It does not extend to strategic, programmatic, or personnel decisions, which remain with the Board or the properly appointed Executive Director.

---

## 5. Preservation of Governance Records, Institutional Knowledge, Research Records, and Critical Credentials

### 5.1 Governance Records

The GitHub repository at `github.com/restore280/governance` is the authoritative record of all governance documents, board consents, and companion policies. All directors have write access to this repository as a condition of their board service.

### 5.2 Institutional Knowledge

A Human designated by the Executive Director shall maintain a written operations reference covering, at minimum: the video production pipeline and its current state, the publishing calendar and its rationale, active partnership and funding relationships, and the status of pending regulatory filings. This reference shall be stored in a location accessible to the Board Chair, not solely on the Executive Director's personal devices or accounts. Until a dedicated location is established, the Corporation's quarterly reporting requirements under its companion policies serve as the interim mechanism for keeping the Board Chair current on this information.

### 5.3 Critical Credentials and Access

Published research materials and their source records are maintained in the Corporation's designated research archive.

The Corporation maintains an inventory of accounts, systems, and access credentials material to its operations. That inventory, and the credentials themselves, are held in a password manager, not in this document or in the governance repository. The Executive Director is the primary owner of that password manager, and the Board Chair holds designated emergency access to it. A Human designated by the Executive Director shall keep the inventory current and shall confirm at each annual governance review that the Board Chair's emergency access remains functional. This inventory also records, for each communication platform in use, the administrative arrangement required by the Communication Platform Administration Policy.

## 6. Continuity of Essential Financial, Legal, Compliance, and Technology Functions

### 6.1 Financial

The Treasurer function is responsible for banking, the quarterly financial summary, and budget presentation under Bylaws Article VIII, whether or not it is held jointly with the Executive Director. Continuity is addressed through the secondary signer arrangement in Section 4.2 and, once the Corporation retains one, a bookkeeper or accountant with standing access to financial records independent of any single director.

### 6.2 Legal and Compliance

The Corporation's compliance calendar (EIN, Form 5768, Form 1023-EZ, annual Form 990, Delaware annual report, any foreign qualification and charitable registration renewals) shall be maintained in a location accessible to all directors, not solely to the Executive Director. Once outside counsel is retained, counsel's contact information and scope of engagement shall be added to Section 5.2's operations reference.

### 6.3 Technology

Hosting, domain, and email infrastructure access is covered in Section 5.3. Personal production tooling used by the Executive Director in content creation is outside the scope of this plan.

---

## 7. Review

This plan shall be reviewed at each annual governance review under Bylaws Section 7.5, and within 15 days of notification of any of the following: a change in who holds the Executive Director or Treasurer office, a change in Board composition, the opening of a new financial account, or the engagement of outside counsel or an accountant. Each review shall confirm that the credentials inventory described in Section 5.3 remains current and that the Board Chair's emergency access remains functional.

---

## 8. Current Limitation

At a three-person Board, if the Executive Director and the Board Chair are simultaneously unavailable, the remaining director(s) shall act under Sections 3.2 and 3.3 and this plan's other provisions, without a designated additional contact. This Section shall be revised once the Board grows beyond three directors or once outside counsel, an accountant, or staff are engaged.
