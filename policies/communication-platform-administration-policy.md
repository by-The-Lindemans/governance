# COMMUNICATION PLATFORM ADMINISTRATION POLICY

*restore280 Institute — Adopted by the Board of Directors by Written Consent, effective October 1, 2026*

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy governs the administration of communication platforms used by the Corporation's directors and employees to conduct Corporation business. Its purpose is to ensure that no individual's administrative control over a communication platform undermines a requirement, found elsewhere in the Corporation's governance documents, that a deliberation, review, or vote occur without a particular director present, or that an interested director recuse from a discussion. Current examples of such requirements include the independence confirmation conducted without the Executive Director present under Bylaws Section 3.3, the Executive Director compensation determination made by the Independent Directors alone under Bylaws Section 8.7, and recusal on a material conflict under the Conflict of Interest Policy and Bylaws Section 3.12(e). This policy applies equally to any other current or future provision of the Corporation's governance documents imposing a similar requirement.

This policy applies to any communication platform where: (a) the platform is used by more than one director or employee to conduct or discuss Corporation business; and (b) the platform provides an administrative role with the technical ability to access, view, export, or monitor communications beyond what an ordinary participant can access.

---

## 2. Default Administration

Administrative duties for a communication platform within the scope of this policy shall be held by the Executive Director, where doing so is consistent with Section 3.

---

## 3. Conflict-Free Communication Requirement

Where the Executive Director's administrative access to a communication platform would place the Executive Director in a position to access, view, or monitor a communication that a governance document requires to occur without the Executive Director present, or from which the Executive Director is required to recuse, the Executive Director shall not hold administrative duties for that platform, or for the specific channel, group, or space within it where such communications occur.

---

## 4. Escalation

Where Section 3 precludes the Executive Director from holding administrative duties for a communication platform, or a portion of it:

(a) The Board Chair shall hold those administrative duties, provided doing so does not itself create a conflict under Section 3 with respect to the Board Chair; or

(b) if the Board Chair is also precluded under subsection (a), a Human designated by the Executive Director shall hold those administrative duties, provided the designation does not create any further conflict under Section 3, and provided the designation is subsequently approved by the Board.

---

## 5. Standard for Compliance

An administrative arrangement satisfies this policy only if the administrator does not have the practical technical ability to access, view, export, or monitor the content of communications from which that administrator is required to be excluded under Section 3. Where a platform's own features make this impossible to achieve for a required purpose, an alternative communication platform or administrative arrangement shall be used for that purpose instead.

---

## 6. Documentation and Review

The administrative arrangement for each communication platform in current use, and the basis for that arrangement under this policy, shall be recorded in the credentials inventory maintained under the Succession and Continuity Plan, rather than in this policy or the governance repository. This arrangement shall be reviewed at each annual governance review under Bylaws Section 7.5, and within 15 days of notification of the adoption of any new communication platform or any change in who administers an existing one.

---

## 7. Amendment

This policy may be amended by majority Board action pursuant to Article VII of the Bylaws, consistent with Article X of the Bylaws.

---

*Adopted by the Board of Directors by Written Consent, effective October 1, 2026*
